# Cluster box accuracy

Gate: median per-object worst-axis resting-box error within 10% over matched easy-tier objects (s01-s04, s06; s05 unscored, no clean-board origin). Dev easy tier is 19 objects, so the gate is the 10th. Metric: `evaluation/metrics.py`, `gates.segmentation.cluster_bbox_error_max`. Evidence: `benchmark/results/p2_3b_dev.json` (`benchmark/run_lift.py --score`), `_b` runs, dev only.

| Cluster | Easy median | Easy ≤10% | All-dev median | All-dev ≤10% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Visual hull | 36.4% | 3/19 | 35.3% | 5/54 |
| Sparse SfM points | 18.7% | 7/19 | 18.7% | 16/55 |
| Trimmed points (`trim_points_per_side: 2`) | 8.9% | 10/19 | 13.2% | 21/55 |
| Fitted primitive from trimmed points (`primitive_fit`) | 4.3% | 15/19 | 7.3% | 33/55 |

`ObjectInstance.point_cluster_path` holds the fitted cluster (human decision 2026-10-29; supersedes the sparse-point choice); `video2sim eval --stage segmentation` scores it. Trimmed points sit beside it (`segmentation/clusters/obj_NNN_points.npy`), hull as `obj_NNN_hull.npy`; `segmentation/fit.json` records kind, size, mask cost, and whether the cluster is the primitive, its union with points, or points alone. Through `SegmentationStage` (18 runs in `runs_instances/`, no hosted call): same 4.3% easy median, 15/19 (`benchmark/results/p2_6b_instances.json`); eval with board-frame alignment: recall 94.9%, same box numbers (`benchmark/results/p2_eval_segmentation_dev.json`).

Two faults, from per-axis signed errors of the sparse clusters: (1) stray points set the box — clusters read too large (flat boxes 15-22 mm too tall from 1-4% floating points; s06 phone box 72% too wide from one isolated point). These are short SfM tracks triangulated from nearby frames with poor depth, kept by the 90%-of-40-views vote on short `_b` arcs. A box is set by outliers, so a few suffice. (2) Without strays, boxes read too small — points exist only on visible textured surfaces; plain edges, white parts, hidden faces have none (6 of 9 failing easy objects under after trimming: mugs −12%/−13% on handles, action camera −11%, deodorant cap −33% at 20 points, car height −14%, in-ear box −12%). Masks carry the missing extent (IoU 0.96 vs projected ground-truth boxes); the hull is loose (~35% large) along viewing directions the short arc never saw.

Fixes in effect order: (1) trimming 2 outermost points per side of the six resting-box faces (axes from `cv2.minAreaRect`, ground-truth convention; clusters under `6k+min_points` points untouched): 18.7% → 8.9% easy. Trimming 4 reached 7.5% but increased undershoot, so 2 was kept. (2) Primitive fit (`stages/primitive_fit.py`): upright box / upright elliptic cylinder / sphere, standing on the table, seeded random walk from the trimmed-point box; lowest mask-outline disagreement wins; outlines on other tracks' masks cost nothing (occlusion); edge-touching views skipped; cost above `max_cost` (0.10) yields union of primitive and points. Effect: 8.9% → 4.3% easy, 13.2% → 7.3% all-dev. Box/round objects land at 0-7%; box-only fitting medians 21% because boxes cannot match round outlines. Cost: 1-12 s/scene on M4 (300 steps/kind, quarter-resolution masks). Remaining easy failures are white-on-white parts both masks and SfM miss (in-ear box tab −18% all methods; mug handles, s02 union 32% off vs 12% points-only) — a segmentation deficit, not recoverable in the lift.

Alternatives measured on the same runs (easy median): extend-to-table 18.9%; track-length ≥4/5 or reprojection/triangulation cuts 9.6-12.2% (remove good edge points with strays); `min_agreement` 1.0 10.1% but drops 5 matched objects elsewhere; statistical/radius/connected-group/erosion variants 12-73%; all-frames 18.7% (no change); vote-only-in-observed-frames 75%; clip-hull-to-points 26%.

Limits: thresholds tuned on the same 19 scored objects (trim passes by 1, fit by 5); easy test scenes had no runs at measurement time. Primitive clusters hold synthetic outlines (box corners / cylinder rings / sphere grid), not observed samples — registration reads `obj_NNN_points.npy` + `fit.json` (outside typed output). Measured on `_b` arcs (45-130° azimuth); full orbits untracked (no upload yet). Ground-truth sizes are folding-rule triplets; positions are ~1 cm off in places (size metric unaffected).

# Segmentation recall: occlusion voting, small tracks, sliver splits, duplicates

Four tracking-to-cluster settings (`stages/lift.py`, `stages/track_repair.py`; `segmentation.lift` and `segmentation.tracking.repair` in `configs/default.yaml`), motivated by the s15 tabletop (5 medium-tier objects): video_a lost two cube-sized objects at the lift, video_b one, and a fixed-lift rerun exported the coffee can twice. All evidence replayed from run caches, no hosted calls.

Occlusion-aware voting (`occlusion_aware: true`): the voter required `hits/seen ≥ 0.9` per voxel, counting occluded views fully against the object. Objects hidden in more than ~1 in 10 sampled views could never pass; at 5 mm voxels with `min_voxels: 20`, a 5 cm cube is only ~60 voxels pre-filter. The dropped s15 tracks had 98-132 frames at ~8k px median masks — agreement failure, not coverage. With the flag, voxels landing on another track's mask but not the own mask excuse that view (same as missing-mask views). Single-track behavior is unchanged (combined mask equals own mask). Effect on s15: video_a kept `[0,1,2,7]` → `[0,1,2,3,4,7]`, video_b `[0,1,2,4]` → all five; recovered clusters are cube-sized (400-950 points, 5-8.5 cm vs 5/5.5 cm truth). Segmentation eval on s15 (test split, confirmation only): recall 0.6 → 1.0, all 5 matched within ~1 cm, false positives flat at 1; box/switch criteria `no_data` (medium tier, no `tracks/`). Dev-wide replay over 12 runs with saved tracks (59 tracks): kept/dropped sets byte-identical — no regressions. Identity switches (`benchmark/run_tracking.py --score`, `benchmark/results/p2_2_tracking.json`) stay 0.0 raw and repaired; the lift runs after repair and never touches masks.

Size-adaptive thresholds (mechanism on, defaults neutral): tracks under `small_mask_px` (15000) median pixels vote with `small_min_agreement`/`small_min_voxels`, both still 0.9/20 in `default.yaml`. The suggested 0.75/8 kept identical s15 sets but admitted noisier voxels (dev median cluster size error 0.143 → 0.183) and risks rescuing hand/arm fragments currently removed by `min_voxels: 20`. Pinned by unit tests (`tests/test_lift.py`); adjust only for scenes where small tracks fail on agreement despite the occlusion fix.

Sliver-split guard (`sliver_frac: 0.2`): the s15 rerun split the coffee can 1→{1,7} (centers 7 mm apart; merge correctly refused at 28 cm footprint separation). Frames 78-107 show the can nearly fully occluded — a 4k px sliver (vs 63k px elsewhere) with its bottom edge 447 px higher, placing its footprint ~28 cm away. Mask feet below 0.2× track median no longer seed groups; those frames rejoin the temporally nearest group. Replay: split gone, 5 objects, one can.

Post-lift duplicate drop (`dup_dist_m: 0.02`): centers within 2 cm keep the larger cluster; the smaller drops as `duplicate of track N` in scene flags. Distinct objects cannot sit this close (touching 5 cm cubes are 5 cm center-to-center; lid-on-can ~9 cm). Unit-tested (`_duplicate_tracks`); emergent lift geometry does not reliably stage it synthetically.

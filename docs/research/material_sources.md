# Material table sources (P4.3)

Sourced values live in `configs/material_table.yaml`; unsourced entries there remain wide placeholders (D-019). Source PDFs are in `docs/sources/` (git-ignored). Values below were read by hand from the stated locations.

Verified densities: PLA solid resin 1240 kg/m³ (ASTM D1505; NatureWorks Ingeo 4043D TDS NW4043D_051815V1, p.1). Wood at 12% moisture 336-784 kg/m³ for specific gravity 0.30-0.70 (560 at 0.50); ovendry most species 320-720, extremes 160 (balsa) to 1040+ (USDA Wood Handbook FPL-GTR-190, ch.4 Table 4-6a). Engineered wood specific gravity (same, ch.12 Table 12-1/12-6): plywood 0.4-0.6, particleboard 0.6-0.8, MDF 0.7-0.9 (mills 0.71-0.95), hardboard 0.9-1.0. Marks' Standard Handbook 10th ed., Table 6.1.5 (PDF pp.348-350) and plastics Table 6.12.1 specific-gravity row (ASTM D792): steel 7703-7832, aluminium 2643 avg (2.55-2.80), glass 2580-2595 avg (common 2.40-2.80, crystal 2.90-3.00), cork 240 (0.22-0.26), paper 929 avg solid sheet (0.70-1.15), leather 945 avg (0.86-1.02), rubber 946 avg caoutchouc (0.92-0.96; filled "rubber goods" 1.0-2.0), cotton fibre 1520 (wool 1.32, nylon 1.14, silk 1.35 — fibres, not fabrics), ABS 1.02-1.08, acrylic 1.17-1.20, nylon 6 1.12-1.14, polycarbonate 1.20, polyethylene 0.918-0.965 (LD→HD), polystyrene 1.04-1.05.

Friction: dry smooth wood on hard smooth surfaces kinetic 0.3-0.5, static higher but unstated (Wood Handbook ch.4 p.4-17). Marks' Table 3.2.4/3.2.6 (PDF pp.130-131): oak-on-oak 0.62 static/0.48 sliding parallel, 0.54/0.32 perpendicular; dry steel-on-polymer nylon 0.4, Plexiglas/PVC/polystyrene 0.5, LD polyethylene 0.4, HD 0.15; natural cork on glass 0.52, on pine 0.49. All pairs are metal/wood-on-wood/polymer-on-steel — none is object-on-tabletop. Restitution: Marks' gives only the definition (e = √(h/H)); no values.

Restitution measurements: Marinack et al., Tribology Trans. 56(4) 2013, 572-580 — 4.76/6.35 mm spheres onto thin (6.35 mm, mostly S7 steel) plates at ~2.1-3.1 m/s, rebound from high-speed video, Fig.3 p.577 eye-read (±0.02): glass-on-glass 0.93-0.94; glass-on-steel 0.88-0.93 (4.76 mm; 0.84-0.86 at 6.35 mm); polybutadiene rubber on steel 0.74-0.78; brass/chrome/S2/low-carbon steels on steel ~0.64-0.76; tungsten carbide on steel ~0.52-0.55. Metals fall with impact speed; e falls as sphere-to-plate-thickness ratio rises. Skórski et al., Materials 14(19) 5638 (2021): 149 g steel ram onto clamped 120 mm composite laminates (carbon/Kevlar-carbon/glass/wood-veneer epoxy), fitted e at zero drop 0.84-0.88, −0.0006-0.0008 per mm drop. Composite laminates match no table class.

Missing: restitution for PLA/ABS, wood, cardboard, ceramic, paper, foam, fabric, and for any object on a tabletop — only glass/rubber/metal spheres on steel plates at 2-3 m/s exist. Friction on a tabletop for every class. Density for infilled printed PLA (resin value is an upper bound), foam, ceramic, foil-wrapped objects, board cardboard, and finished fabric items.
